the viva that scales

Every student defends. Every teacher hears. Offline, on phones they already own.

teach → defend → see → adjust

The teacher certifies the question sets — human-curated, cryptographically signed — students defend their understanding in Socratic dialogue, fully offline, nothing leaving the device. The teacher sees which concepts held and which slipped — the class's comprehension shape — before the next lecture, because from now on, they always will.

An AI that makes you reason — not one that reasons for you.

A coach, not a vending machine; a scaffold, not a crutch — built so you need it less over time, not more.

rules we refuse to break

Rules a rival can't copy without breaking its own business.

1

Never-rank, enforced in code — class views are aggregate-only; a class smaller than five shows the teacher nothing; there is no score in the system to rank by.

2

Teacher-certified content — every question set is human-curated and cryptographically signed before a student ever sees it.

3

User-owned data — the student's work lives in an encrypted vault on their own device, exportable by them under India's data law, never a company data-lake.

Privacy is by construction, not policy: all inference on-device, no network calls — shown, not promised.

the constitution

“Be the air society breathes.”— p.1 of the notebook where this company started

“Any layer a person must use to live, they must be able to leave — with everything.”

  • 01Never rank the person — dignity at the floor, competition only for distinction.
  • 02Sovereign by construction — your device, your data, leave with everything.
  • 03Loyal to the human it touches — measured by outcomes, never attention.
  • 04Makes you reason — never reasons for you.
  • 05No engagement machinery — no streaks, badges, or leaderboards; checkable by absence.
  • 06Teacher-in-the-loop — a human certifies everything a student sees.

The record is your evidence to yourself first — you cannot fool yourself about what you actually understand.

for the curious

The stack, and what we're testing next.

Student inference runs entirely on the student's own phone — a ~4B open model executed through our own llama.cpp FFI — so a session makes no network calls and costs our servers nothing. The server is Rust over Postgres; it generates the teacher's certified content and signs it with Ed25519 before any student sees it, so cost grows with lectures and never with enrolment.

The repository README carries that architecture in full, alongside the six rules above. What we're testing next: the demos three campuses have committed to hosting in the new academic session, then pilots — and whether a teacher, shown the comprehension shape, actually changes the next lecture.
